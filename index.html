THE ART OF STAYING SMALL

A quick briefing.

Hold left or right on the game or the control pad to move. Slide to the other half to change direction.

Lift your finger to stop and turn invisible for a moment. Your cloak takes 3 seconds to recharge. Watch the meter.

Dodge the falling debris. Survive for points and earn 25 extra for each close call. One hit ends your run.

On a keyboard: ← → or A D to move. Release to cloak. P to pause.
